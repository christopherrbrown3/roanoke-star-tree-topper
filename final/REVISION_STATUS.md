# Superseded assembly candidate

The current model is in [../print_in_place](../print_in_place/DESIGN_REPORT.md): 130 individual tube sections and an integral mount, made in one two-color A1/AMS lite print. This folder retains the older continuous-insert assembly candidate and its validation history. Its path measurements remain the source for the new geometry.

Landmark geometry is not verified or accepted as exact. On 12 September 2026 the continuous-insert candidate here was rebuilt in Blender and passed its digital mesh, intersection, fit and 3MF checks. See [the accuracy audit](../research/ACCURACY_AUDIT.md) and the historical [design report](DESIGN_REPORT.md).
