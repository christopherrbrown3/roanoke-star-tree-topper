This report describes the latest single-print design generated by `scripts/prepare_a1_print_in_place.py` and `scripts/build_a1_print_in_place_blender.py`. It supersedes the earlier research recommendations for snap-fit cassettes, separate face masks, and attachment screws. The current target is one fused, two-color print with no post-print assembly, using a Bambu Lab A1 with AMS Lite.

## Design intent

The star is printed face-down. The white material forms 130 separate rounded capsule inlays in the first 1.0 mm of the part; the dark material forms the surrounding face and continues into a 4.0 mm structural body. The two materials fuse at the layer interface during the same print. There are no loose tube parts, carriers, face screws, LEDs, wiring, or later installation operations.

Six illuminated paths remain individually represented, now constructed as exact parallel inward offsets of the outer candidate path. Their model section counts are exactly 30, 30, 30, 20, 10, and 10; these are not verified historical tube counts. Long straight runs use a nominal 1.2 mm dark gap; the measured minimum separation between any two white sections is 1.2000 mm. Straight-edge clear gaps within the three pairs are 1.937, 1.992, and 1.865 mm; the wider gaps between pairs are 4.195 and 4.267 mm. White width is 1.8 mm. Ends are rounded in the XY outline by the capsule geometry, not by a post-print bevel.

The geometry is an appearance reconstruction from photographs. The outline and the interruption schedule are manufacturing approximations, not an archival fabrication drawing. The all-white target remains six visible tracks in three close pairs.

## Dimensions and mount

The finished star body is 190.960 × 199.997 mm in X/Y. The complete dark body, including its rear mount, is 50.836 mm deep in Z. The design is within the A1's 256 × 256 × 256 mm build envelope when placed flat.

The tree mount is integral with the dark body and grows upward from the rear surface:

| Feature | Value |
|---|---:|
| Sleeve axis | Star Y axis |
| Sleeve openings | y = −32 to +48 mm |
| Sleeve length | 80 mm |
| Lower circular-mandrel equivalent | Ø34 mm |
| Upper circular-mandrel equivalent | Ø22 mm |
| Minimum sleeve wall | 2.4 mm |
| Bore centre depth | z = 23.4 mm |
| Bore roof | 45° house/teardrop facets |
| Tie-hole section | 5 mm diamond diagonal, at y = −18 and +22 mm |

The bore is a flat-bottomed house profile with vertical sides and two 45° roof facets. It contains the corresponding circular mandrel equivalent while avoiding a horizontal circular ceiling. The outer sleeve bottom starts at z = 3.8 mm and overlaps the 4.0 mm body by 0.2 mm, making the mount one connected dark solid. The tapered sleeve footprint is fully behind the star silhouette in front projection; the reported outside area is 0 mm².

The tie holes are transverse diamond openings through the sleeve. Their current script value is 5 mm diagonal, not the 4 mm size considered in an earlier audit. The holes are intentionally roofed rather than circular so their upper edges do not require support. The sleeve fit has not been tested against a real tree leader; a leader smaller than the nominal bore is expected to be needed.

## Printing assumptions

Use the A1 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.20 mm layer height with two PLA materials mapped through AMS Lite: dark navy for the body and white for the capsule inlays. Keep the plate face-down as exported, with the color face on the build plate and the integral mount rising in +Z. The face has no downward cavities; the sleeve floor is fused to the backing; and the roof facets are 45° in the primary X–Z section. These are geometric support-minimization assumptions, not a successful slicer result.

The white inlays are only 1.0 mm deep and are flush with the bed-facing front surface after printing. A first-layer expansion or elephant-foot setting can reduce the 1.2000 mm closest gap, so the first sliced preview should be checked at the paired paths and at the capsule ends. The 130 sections add travel moves, but sections of the same color can be printed together: they do not require 130 AMS changes per layer. Color changes and purge are confined to the first five layers at the stated layer height. Confirm that the slicer preserves every white section and its surrounding dark material.

Start with four wall loops, five top and bottom shell layers, 15% gyroid infill, and the prime tower enabled. Use a clean, compatible build plate; no brim is proposed initially because of the broad contact area. A small outer brim is an option if tip lifting occurs. These settings have not yet been sliced or physically tested. Use compatible PLA for both colors; a mixed PLA/PETG interface is not the intended fused construction.

- white inlays: watertight, consistent winding, 130 connected solids, 24,704 triangles, zero zero-area or duplicate triangles, 4.8961 cm³;
- fused material envelope: watertight and one connected solid, 696 triangles, zero zero-area triangles, 86.2161 cm³;
- material overlap: approximately 2 × 10⁻¹⁵ mm³, within numerical precision;
- mandrel test: 0 mm³ overlap with the recorded 0.05 mm radial allowance;
- front projection of the mount outside the star: 0 mm²;
- solid PLA mass estimate: 106.91 g. A sliced A1 print will normally weigh less because the estimate is based on solid mesh volume rather than a particular wall and infill profile;
- centre of mass: x ≈ 0, y = −1.586 mm, z = 7.727 mm; reported solid-PLA gravity moment is 0.0164 N·m.

`self_intersections.json` reports zero non-adjacent triangle-intersection candidates for both the dark body and white sections. The backing's coplanar Boolean triangulation was repaired through the Manifold kernel, returned to Blender, and checked again without adding any intersection exclusion. The mesh volume change was less than 0.000008 mm³. The exported white front differs from the prepared tube footprints by only 0.00346 mm² across 4896.13 mm², within float export precision.

The generated standard 3MF has one build object, two material volumes, and a successful round-trip mesh validation. The primary color-print deliverable is `roanoke_star_A1.3mf`.

The file `roanoke_star_A1_geometry_reference.stl` is intentionally a one-material outer geometry reference and therefore has no contrasting white tubes. `body_material.stl` and `white_tube_material.stl` are the separate dark and white material meshes for mapping when needed; they are not a post-print assembly instruction. Use the 3MF to preserve the one-object, two-material relationship.

## Limitations and release gates

The Blender build and mesh checks are complete, but no physical print has been made. Bambu Studio validation is deferred at the owner's request. There is no confirmed toolpath, purge estimate, support result, first-layer gap result, or print time. The geometry check's zero overhang area describes the constructed mesh and does not replace inspection of Bambu Preview.

The landmark outline is not verified against a surveyed front elevation. The capsule positions and counts are photo-informed and mirrored/repeated where the close photographs do not establish an exact fabrication schedule. The 34/22 mm mount values describe nominal circular-mandrel-equivalent space, not a tested universal tree fit. No load, flex, branch retention, or seasonal handling test has been performed.

Before calling this a print release, inspect the two-material 3MF in Bambu Studio with the A1 0.4 profile and 0.20 mm process, confirm the face-down orientation, confirm every first-layer white capsule and the dark-to-white transitions, inspect the 45° sleeve roofs and tie holes in Preview, and save the sliced project locally. Do not infer physical reliability from the Blender watertightness checks alone.
