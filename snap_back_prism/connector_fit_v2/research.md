[Formlabs' snap-fit guide](https://formlabs.com/blog/designing-3d-printed-snap-fit-enclosures/) recommends considering FDM layer orientation, longer compliant arms, lower engagement for easier assembly, and separate locating lugs. Its enclosure example primarily targets SLA/SLS, so its dimensions are not adopted as an FDM guarantee.

Recommendation for this topper: test a longer planar cantilever with one broad ramped hook and a simple wall window. Keep the arm within the printed layer plane and preserve face-down printing. The new tests use a 30 mm arm, an 8 mm catch, and separate guides. One and two dots identify 0.30 mm and 0.50 mm engagement. These dimensions are our engineering trial, informed by the sources, and must be judged by a new physical fit test.

Simply scaling every dimension up would also increase stiffness. The revised layout increases arm length while controlling its bending thickness and clearance. This is an inference from the cantilever geometry, not a claim that these exact samples have already been proven.
