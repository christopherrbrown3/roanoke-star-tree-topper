# Spacing of the three paired outlines

The existing spacing is retained after review of the printed example, the source paths, and the primary aerial photograph. The three groups are already separated by clear gaps approximately twice the size of the gaps within each pair.

| Measurement, normal to a straight run | Current model |
|---|---:|
| Tube width | 1.800 mm |
| Clear gaps within pairs | 1.937 / 1.992 / 1.865 mm |
| Clear gaps between pairs | 4.195 / 4.267 mm |
| Mean centerline spacing within pairs | 3.731 mm |
| Mean centerline spacing between pairs | 6.031 mm |
| Between-pair / within-pair centerline ratio | 1.616 |

These are perpendicular distances, so a diagonal edge has the same gap as a horizontal one. Tip-to-tip distances are larger because adjacent offset lines intersect at a corner. The 1.20 mm breaks along individual tubes are a separate measurement.

## Photographic cross-check

The [City aerial image](https://www.roanokeva.gov/ImageRepository/Document?documentID=13604) remains the primary six-path tracing source. A fresh review used its original 2600 × 1461 pixel image. Two approximate vertical cross-sections through the near-horizontal shoulders locate the six illuminated centerlines at:

| Image x coordinate | Approximate image y coordinates, outside to inside | Centerline ratio |
|---|---|---:|
| 490 px | 651, 660, 675, 683, 697, 706 px | 1.67 |
| 612 px | 652, 660, 674, 683, 696, 704 px | 1.62 |

These pixel spot-checks have roughly 1–2 pixel reading uncertainty, include glow and interruptions, and are not a metric camera calibration. They corroborate the existing fit's grouping; they do not establish surveyed dimensions. Comparing adjacent lines locally reduces the effect of foreshortening, but does not remove perspective. The close [Commons neon photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mill_Mountain_Star_Neon_Lights.JPG) and daylight aerial views were also reviewed for the paired pattern and backing bands. Their oblique views and different lighting modes do not provide better absolute gap measurements.

The supplied research's 13 / 17 / 16 pixel within-pair intervals and 27 / 28 pixel between-pair intervals imply a **1.793 centerline ratio**, not a clear-gap ratio. Its source cross-section and camera correction are not reproducible from the supplied notes. Both estimates support wider spaces between pairs; the evidence does not justify enlarging the current geometry to one exact ratio.

## Print evidence and design choice

The owner reported a successful two-color STL print and supplied a photograph of the unengraved version. The photograph shows the paired outlines, but its perspective and lighting are unsuitable for dimensioning either the print or the landmark. It is retained privately and is not redistributed here.

At a 0.4 mm nozzle width, the clear gaps between paired tube groups are over ten nozzle widths across. The existing tube spacing is therefore retained. A wider gap can be an aesthetic preference, but would not be an established correction to the landmark geometry. The source edge directions, exact parallelism, and overall envelope remain unchanged.

## White backing bands

The subsequent face treatment adds three white bands beneath the tube pattern. Each band's outer and inner boundaries sit 2.2 mm from the corresponding tube centerlines. The resulting **clear gaps between the white bands are 1.595 and 1.667 mm**; these are different from the 4.195 and 4.267 mm distances between tube edges. A 0.6 mm dark keyline surrounds each tube so the white tube sections remain visible against the white bands. All visible regions are flush to the print bed and use the same two materials.

The 2.2 mm band margin and 0.6 mm keyline are choices for a readable, printable miniature. They are not measured dimensions of the landmark's backing panels. The actual tube positions are preserved.
