# Original-plan search — 10–12 September 2026

**No dimensioned original Star elevation, complete shop drawing, or modern measured elevation was located in the material examined.** This does not establish that no plans survive. There is still no archival basis for assigning exact interior/exterior angles, notch positions, or nested frame offsets.

## City of Roanoke Engineering

The [Engineering Division](https://www.roanokeva.gov/354/Engineering) identifies archived survey/plan records, GIS links to parcels, and Facilities' shop-submission and photograph files. The public [EngMapsandPlans Laserfiche repository](https://laserfiche.roanokeva.gov/WebLink/Browse.aspx?dbid=0&repo=EngMapsandPlans) is searchable without contacting staff.

The General Search term `star` returned 92 entries, including both folders and documents. All five result pages were reviewed by title. Most concern subdivisions, businesses, or property surveys. The potentially relevant **City of Roanoke (46)** was opened and visually inspected: [document 11101](https://laserfiche.roanokeva.gov/WebLink/DocView.aspx?id=11101&dbid=0&repo=EngMapsandPlans) is a one-page land-acquisition plat, not the Star.

The term `Mill Mountain` returned 153 entries; the first 100 titles were screened. Results include park surveys, a transmitter tower, zoo buildings, roads, the Greenway, and subdivisions. [Mill Mountain Park, document 19484](https://laserfiche.roanokeva.gov/WebLink/DocView.aspx?id=19484&dbid=0&repo=EngMapsandPlans), was opened and visually inspected: drawing **2192-B** is a park land/topographic survey, not a front elevation of the Star. Search-title screening does not prove that every sheet or unindexed file lacks a Star detail. Internal Facilities records may not be represented in this public repository.

The historic nomination identifies parcel **4050306**. That is a historical identifier, not independently verified as a current parcel number. The current Engineering page lists Luke E. Pugh, City Engineer, at 540-853-5201. No call or message was made.

## DHR and National Park Service

The [DHR nomination for resource 128-0352](https://www.dhr.virginia.gov/VLR_to_transfer/PDFNoms/128-0352_Roanoke_Star_1999_Final_Nomination.pdf) is an actual 17-page PDF. Its last exhibits are site/location maps, visually checked as images.

- PDF page 15 is printed exhibit page 11, **Exhibit A**, a tax parcel map.
- PDF page 16 is printed exhibit page 17, **Exhibit G**, the revised 1950 Mill Mountain Park survey.
- The available file does **not** include Exhibits B–F. Their contents are unknown; the gap is a reason to request the complete file, not evidence that those exhibits contain engineering drawings.
- The photo list specifies Anne Stuart Beckett, 6 July 1999, negative **17627**, and north / south-and-west elevation views.
- The acknowledgments name Ken Harris and Bill Liles of City Engineering.

The [NPS asset record, NRIS 99001375](https://npgallery.nps.gov/AssetDetail/NRIS/99001375), links to nomination and photo assets. At retrieval, both links returned the same one-page **NOT DIGITIZED** placeholder, rather than the full files. The two locally retained PDFs are explicitly named `*_NOT_DIGITIZED.pdf`. They provide no geometry. The [DHR Archives](https://www.dhr.virginia.gov/programs/dhr-archives/) are a lead for the complete physical/oversized record.

## Virginia Room holdings

The [vertical-files finding aid](https://www.virginiaroom.org/digital/files/original/13/1792/VerticalFiles.2.pdf) lists these actual topical files:

- PDF page 10: **Business & Industry — VA — Roanoke — Roy C. Kinsey Sign Company**.
- PDF page 10: **Business & Industry — VA — Roanoke — Roanoke Iron & Bridge Works**.
- PDF page 52: **Mountains — VA — Roanoke — Mill Mountain**.

The index confirms files exist; their contents have not been inspected and cannot be described as holding Star plans. The [maps/architectural-plans finding aid](https://www.virginiaroom.org/digital/file/7400) includes 1941 **Observation Tower for Roanoke, Virginia** sheets and a Roy C. Kinsey residence drawing. Neither is a 1949 Star drawing. The Dr. John Kern finding aid was also checked without locating an explicit Star entry.

Edward C. Moomaw's [1982 account, “How the star was turned on”](https://www.virginiaroom.org/digital/files/original/67/6354/JHSWV_11_02_1982.pdf), was visually examined in full at PDF pages 90–92 / printed pages 88–90. It identifies Roy C. Kinsey Sign Company and **Robert L. Little** of Roanoke Iron & Bridge Works, whereas DHR uses **Robert R. Little**. It contains history and a photograph, not a dimensioned elevation. Search both initials.

## Modern studies and fabrication history

[WDBJ's 2023 report](https://www.wdbj7.com/2023/05/10/roanoke-considers-restoration-or-replacement-mill-mountain-star/) and [WSLS's May 2023 report](https://www.wsls.com/news/local/2023/05/18/roanoke-leaders-to-consider-refurbishment-plan-for-mill-mountain-star/) establish a contemporary restoration discussion. The reported 2019 inspection and 2021 internal study remain leads: their complete reports, appendices, and dimensioned attachments were not retrieved. Public Parks advisory-board pages and indexed WordPress media did not expose those engineering reports in the searches made.

The [WDBJ interview transcript preserved by Virginia Tech](https://scholar.lib.vt.edu/VA-news/WDBJ-7/script_archives/99/1199/112899/112899.m.htm) describes assembly flat on the airport ground, disassembly, and re-erection on the mountain. This supports seeking fabrication/erection records. It does not itself show a drawing or establish an angle.
