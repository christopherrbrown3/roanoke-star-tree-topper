## City Engineering / Facilities / Parks

Contact route: [City Engineering](https://www.roanokeva.gov/354/Engineering), with routing to Facilities and Parks as appropriate.

**Subject: Roanoke Star — dimensioned front elevation and fabrication records**

I am researching the geometry of the 1949 Roanoke / Mill Mountain Star for a small physical replica. I am particularly interested in the interior and exterior angles of the ten-vertex outlines and the offsets between its three frames.

Could you identify any surviving 1949 fabrication/shop drawings, erection drawings, later as-built surveys, or measured front elevations? Relevant names include Roy C. Kinsey Sign Company, Roanoke Iron & Bridge Works, and Robert R. / Robert L. Little. The historic DHR identifier is 128-0352; the 1999 nomination credits Ken Harris and Bill Liles of City Engineering.

I would also appreciate the geometry-related drawings and appendices from the reported 2019 structural inspection, 2021 internal study, and subsequent rehabilitation planning, if available. I have checked the public Engineering Laserfiche index; the Mill Mountain Park drawing 2192-B is a site survey rather than the needed elevation.

The most useful details would be overall frontal width and height, shoulder/notch heights, point coordinates, member widths, and tube-centerline offsets for all three frames. Please indicate whether dimensions describe structural edges or illuminated tube paths. Public catalogs give conflicting width-related figures: approximately 84.5 feet in Smithsonian inventory IAS VA000441 versus a 1062 × 1200-inch city art-catalog entry.

Digital copies or a finding-aid reference would be welcome. Please advise about reproduction fees before any chargeable work.
