# Reference sources and attribution

Reference originals are linked below rather than redistributed. The public repository contains model renders, geometry diagrams, measurements, and research notes. Historical filenames in the notes identify the material used during analysis; original photographs, photo crops and overlays, and downloaded archival PDFs are excluded.

- City of Roanoke: https://www.roanokeva.gov/1329/Roanoke-Star — published height, three frames and neon description.
- Smithsonian inventory: https://siris-artinventories.si.edu/ipac20/ipac.jsp?booklistformat=&profile=ariall&session=1761P49582NO9.272492&uri=full%3D3100001~%21334963~%210 — approximate overall dimensions.
- DHR nomination: https://www.dhr.virginia.gov/VLR_to_transfer/PDFNoms/128-0352_Roanoke_Star_1999_Final_Nomination.pdf — historical and structural description.
- City aerial image: https://www.roanokeva.gov/ImageRepository/Document?documentID=13604 — primary six-path tracing source. Other city captures use document IDs 13600–13603.
- Ben Schumin: https://www.schuminweb.com/photo_features/aerial-view-of-the-roanoke-star/ — high-resolution 13 June 2024 aerial. Research filename: `schumin_aerial_2024.webp` (not distributed).
- Ben Schumin travel series: https://www.schuminweb.com/life-and-times/adventures-in-tennessee-part-7/ — local images 393, 399, 400, 401, 429 and 430. Photos credited to Ben Schumin.
- Wikimedia: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Roanoke_star.jpg — Ben Schumin, 7 April 2005. The source offers multiple licenses, including the [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) option. Research filename (not distributed): `references/commons_roanoke_star.jpg`.
- Wikimedia: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mill_Mountain_Star_Neon_Lights.JPG — TampAGS for AGS Media, 17 November 2008, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Research filename (not distributed): `references/commons_neon_lights.jpg`.
- Virginia's Blue Ridge: https://www.visitroanokeva.com/things-to-do/attractions/roanoke-star/ — tourism reference and patriotic aerial.
- Edward C. Moomaw, 1982: https://www.virginiaroom.org/digital/files/original/67/6354/JHSWV_11_02_1982.pdf — first-person construction history.

The two Commons source pages identify their authors and Creative Commons terms. Original photographs remain the property of their respective rights holders. No repository-wide license is granted over linked reference material. See [GEOMETRY_REVIEW.md](GEOMETRY_REVIEW.md) for the view-by-view inventory and [ACCURACY_AUDIT.md](ACCURACY_AUDIT.md) for the limits of the reconstruction.

The Creative Commons terms on Ben Schumin's Commons photograph do not extend to every photograph on his website. His [content-licensing page](https://www.schuminweb.com/content-licensing/) distinguishes Creative Commons work from later all-rights-reserved photographs, for which personal use does not include public rehosting. The [City's copyright notice](https://www.roanokeva.gov/site/copyright) also reserves rights in its site content. Source links provide attribution; they do not establish permission to redistribute separately licensed originals.
